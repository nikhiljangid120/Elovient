Integrated Paint Manufacturing ERP Architecture

Scalable, Cloud-Native Architecture Replacing Excel & Unifying Tally Financials

Architecture Status: Production-Ready Design
High-Level System & Data Flow Architecture Event-Driven Microservices + Polyglot Persistence

Functional Requirements Coverage Matrix

01. Inventory Control

  • Multi-Location Tracking: Central plant, regional distribution centers, consignment depots.
  • Batch & Expiry: Automatic lot generation, pot-life / shelf-life monitoring, FEFO dispatching.
  • Automated Reorder: Safety-stock dynamic formulas trigger PO indents before stockouts occur.

02. Production Management

  • BOM & Recipe Linking: Strict formulation controls tied dynamically to active inventory lots.
  • WIP Stage Tracking: High-speed dispersion $\to$ Let-down $\to$ Tinting $\to$ QC release $\to$ Packing.
  • Auto FG Stock Update: Immediate inventory induction and solvent evaporation variance accounting.

03. Sales & Distribution

  • Automated O2C: Order intake $\to$ Credit check $\to$ Pick/Pack $\to$ E-way bill dispatch.
  • Real-Time Stock Visibility: Regional sales reps view exact physical and allocated stock.
  • Tiered Pricing: Distributor, dealer, contractor discounts and seasonal volume rebates.

04. Procurement & Supply Chain

  • Automated Indents: Generated automatically from low-stock alerts and planned production BOMs.
  • Vendor Optimization: Lead time and defect scorecards prevent unreliable supplier contracts.
  • Procure-to-Pay: 3-way match across PO, GRN inspection, and vendor billing.

05. Financial Management

  • Automated Accounting: Operational actions post directly to double-entry general ledger.
  • Batch Costing: Standard vs actual batch cost analysis (raw materials + machine overhead).
  • Tally Integration Bridge: Seamless XML sync preserving historical Tally data without operational stall.

06. Reporting & Analytics

  • Executive Dashboard: Real-time Inventory Turnover, OTIF rates, and product line margins.
  • Batch Genealogy: Comprehensive audit trail from chemical supplier barrel to end customer can.
  • Regulatory Compliance: Automated hazardous chemical handling and MSDS documentation.
